# Contract v3 backlog

Gaps in `bellwether-types` (contract v2) and in the docs that the Phase 2 authors hit while implementing build, profile, and replay. Each entry names who raised it, what the crate does today, and the proposed resolution. Nothing here is decided until it lands as an ADR or a format amendment; until then the interim behaviour stands. Contract v3 opens after M3 (`docs/evolve.md` §10), and every change below is additive or a documented error, never a silent reinterpretation.
