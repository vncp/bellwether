Reading it: on the ledger every key set, even 10 accounts, holds a row in every one of the 1,000 row groups, because the busiest accounts carry entries everywhere and entries are not clustered by account. Without an agg key the per-key sums read every row group (184 MB, or 127 MB of only the key and value columns, and 10^7 rows decoded); with `agg:account@amount{sum,count,min,max}` beside the counted key they read no row group, and every key's rows, null amounts, count, and exact sum equal pyarrow's over the decoded files (the run totals also equal the generator's `truth.json`). Reconciling two 10^7-row copies by account with the exact total of `amount` reads 0 rows per side from the agg keys against 10^7 per side without them, and both find a single amount moved by 0.000001 and name the account; the certificate's `agg` lists the sides an agg key answered, and `R-agg-1` fires on the reconciles that read every row. On `market`, per-symbol daily volume (`(symbol, day)` keys) at 10 keys reads 15 of 216 row groups without an agg key and none with it; the larger sets cover all 500 present pairs (plus absent symbols, answered as zero rows). The market reconcile is not shown: reconcile totals are decimal columns and the market corpus has none (`size` is Int64).
